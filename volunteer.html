Care in every interaction

What we ask of volunteers.

Respect, reliability, and thoughtful boundaries make our work possible.

  • Treat everyone with dignity, respect, and trauma-informed boundaries.
  • Protect confidentiality and follow Aid Room privacy policies.
  • Complete required orientation, safety, and role training.
  • Perform assigned, authorized duties and communicate reliably.
  • Never exchange money, gifts, housing, medication, or private services with clients.
  • Do not photograph or record clients without explicit written authorization.
  • Provide professional advice only when qualified and authorized.
  • Report safety or boundary concerns to your supervisor.

Joining the team

A thoughtful start. A shared purpose.

  1. Reach out

    Email your interests, experience, and availability.

  2. Find the right fit

    We review applications, interview selected applicants, and complete any role-related screening.

  3. Get ready to contribute

    Complete orientation and agree on responsibilities and a schedule.

An application does not guarantee placement. Assignments depend on organizational needs, qualifications, safety requirements, and policies.
