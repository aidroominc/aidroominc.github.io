Our mission

A person beside you.
A path forward.

No one should have to navigate a crisis alone.

Aid Room, Inc. helps individuals experiencing homelessness, mental health challenges, substance use recovery needs, and other crises connect with essential community resources and public-benefit programs.

Through individualized guidance, resource navigation, and one-on-one support, we help people stabilize, rebuild, and move toward long-term independence and well-being.

Practical support

Support for the whole person.

Real life rarely fits into one category. We help people make sense of the resources available and take the next step that fits their needs.

01

Resource navigation

Understand available programs and identify practical next steps.

02

Housing and recovery

Connect with shelters, sober living homes, recovery residences, and housing resources.

03

Benefits guidance

Navigate Medicaid, SNAP, and disability-benefit information and applications.

04

Basic needs

Find food, clothing, hygiene supplies, transportation, and phone services.

05

Health and wellness

Explore mental health resources, treatment centers, and wellness programs.

06

Follow-up support

Address barriers, maintain contact, and find additional resources.

Who we serve

You are more than the challenge you’re facing.

We support adults, youths, and families facing homelessness or housing instability, behavioral health challenges, substance use recovery needs, financial hardship, and other destabilizing circumstances.

Our work centers on restored dignity, reduced isolation, increased trust, greater stability, and renewed hope.

Our leadership

Meet our Board of Directors

Board President

Aaron Johnson

Aaron Johnson has extensive lived leadership and professional experience supporting people affected by mental health challenges, substance use disorders, homelessness, and incarceration through his time as a Certified Colorado Peer and Family Specialist. He has also served in executive and leadership roles with nonprofit organizations, including Men of Standard, Inc. and Summit Healing, Inc. His experience and education in business administration, nonprofit management, program development, and resource navigation guide Aid Room’s mission and responsible growth.

Board Vice President

Aaliyah Meredith

Aaliyah Meredith brings extensive experience in public service, education, peer support, and community engagement. Her experience working closely with urban communities has helped obtain basic necessities, including nutrition and health services for those in need. Aaliyah is especially passionate about serving youth and young adults through mentorship, student guidance, education, and positive encouragement. Her leadership helps Aid Room strengthen its outreach to young adults facing crisis and underserved communities.

Board Secretary

Rebecca Rodriguez

Rebecca Rodriguez brings many years of experience in business leadership, office management, and community-focused service. She has worked with people affected by homelessness, substance use disorders, criminal justice involvement, and barriers to stable housing throughout her career. Rebecca also has valuable knowledge of sober living, transitional housing, and services for women who have experienced victimization. Her administrative skill, professionalism, and compassion support effective governance and accurate organizational records for Aid Room.
